# Tarot card images

The 78 card illustrations in this directory are from the Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot
deck, illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith and first published in 1909–1910.
The images were downloaded from the Wikimedia Commons category
[Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck (Geldard)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(Geldard)),
where the files are marked as public domain.

The original artwork is in the public domain. The Commons files are extracted
and cleaned scans for digital use. Images are stored locally so the app does not
depend on a third-party image host at runtime.
